# 01. Business Requirement Specification (BRS)

This document establishes the high-level business requirement overview for Laya's Cakes & Candles ERP platform.

---

## 1. Business Context
Cakes & Candles is a multi-branch bakery business operating with a centralized production factory (Pudupalayam) and 6 retail branches. The goal of this platform is to unify all business operations (production planning, double-entry stock accounting, logistics, retail POS, custom cake pipelines, CRM, and cost-center bookkeeping) into a single operating platform.

---

## 2. Core Business Domains
1. **Procurement & Supplier Management**: Standardizes ordering raw assets and matching vendor ledger records.
2. **Factory Operations**: Digits daily consolidate indents, recipe explosions, runs, and quality checks.
3. **Inventory Governance & Logistics**: Tracks real-time multi-warehouse balances, transfers, and dispatches.
4. **Retail Commerce (POS)**: Fast counter checkouts with offline capacity and active CRM rewards panel.
5. **Custom Cake Operations**: Visualizes multi-stage baking and decorating lines.
6. **CRM & Marketing**: Customer segments classifications and templates automations.
7. **Finance & HR Admin**: Tracks branch petty expenses and employee attendances shifts.
